NH-Calib: Vehicle-Motion-Constrained Self-Calibration of Onboard Sensor Extrinsics

NH-Calib estimates relative five-degree-of-freedom extrinsics from sensor ego-motion, without calibration targets, direct cross-sensor registration, or ground-truth calibration during estimation.

Paper — manuscript Code — upon publication Explore the method

Authors and affiliations are withheld while the manuscript remains anonymous.

Abstract

Motion-constrained calibration without targets or shared observations.

Extrinsic calibration is essential for fusing vehicle-mounted sensors, but direct registration becomes unreliable when their fields of view or sensing modalities differ. NH-Calib exploits locally planar wheeled- vehicle motion and a relaxed lateral-motion model. On the sensor-own-twist path, each sensor independently estimates X, roll, pitch, and yaw; Z remains unobservable under planar motion, and only relative Y requires cross-sensor association. Associated turning segments are time-aligned, integrated over interpolated common boundaries, and combined through a sum-form relation and robust weighted least squares. The method is evaluated with LiDAR and radar configurations from A2D2, RadarScenes, and Ford Multi-AV. Ground-truth extrinsics are used only for scoring.

Four DoF per sensor X, roll, pitch, and yaw are estimated from each sensor's own metric twist.
Only Y requires association Relative Y is recovered from paired turns with time alignment and common-window integration.
Cross-modal evaluation Validated with LiDAR and radar motion on A2D2, RadarScenes, and Ford Multi-AV.

Overview

The vehicle's shared motion is the calibration signal.

Registration-based calibration relies on scene overlap, and conventional motion calibration couples all parameters to inter-sensor temporal correspondence. This is restrictive for distributed or heterogeneous onboard sensors.

NH-Calib decomposes the problem by observability. Four parameters are estimated independently per sensor, while the remaining relative lateral translation is estimated only after associating compatible turns.

Method

Two stages with a common ego-motion interface.

Timestamped metric twists may come from LiDAR or radar odometry, GNSS/INS, or CAN/IMU. RadarScenes uses Doppler velocity with time-aligned CAN yaw rate because radar point-cloud odometry was unreliable.

01

Per-sensor estimation

Extract turns, align the motion plane, estimate X and yaw from the 2D-aligned twist, then remove the reference-common component.

02

Pairwise Y estimation

Associate turns, estimate timing offsets, interpolate common boundaries, and recover pairwise Y with sum-form integration.

03

Robust integration

Fuse pairwise relative-Y measurements with robust weighted least squares and output reference-relative five-DoF calibration.
