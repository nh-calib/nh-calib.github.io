1 Sensor layouts and published extrinsics

All three layouts are given in the vehicle frame used for scoring: x forward, y left, z up, origin at the rear-axle centre. These are the published values, used only to score — never inside the estimator.

Caution
An earlier internal note described RADAR_3 and RADAR_4 as rear units. That was a naming error; the published x coordinates place all four on the front bumper.

Ford Multi-AV — four HDL-32E

Key point
YELLOW and GREEN carry a real mounting pitch near ±25°. That is a genuine installation value, not an estimation error, and it is why a planar two-dimensional hand-eye variant cannot represent this layout.

2 Ford: all six logs profiled, three used

Six Ford V2 logs were downloaded and profiled before any calibration was run. The profile is computed from the released pose stream alone and decides whether a drive carries enough rotation to be usable at all.

Key point
The three rejected logs are not bad recordings. They are fast, straight drives — exactly the regime in which this method has nothing to work with. A drive with 78° of total turning over 23 km cannot identify a lever arm, and the gate says so before any odometry is run.
Caution
Log1 was nevertheless run end-to-end early in the project, before the gate existed. Two of its four channels lost tracking in the 26–29 m/s section and the four-channel common segment count fell to zero. That outcome is consistent with the gate, and the Log1 numbers were dropped.

4 Measured co-visibility between sensor pairs

The paper argues qualitatively that A2D2 side units share no view with the front ones and that the Ford roof units share a great deal. That claim was measured. Two local maps are placed in the same frame using the published extrinsics — so the measure does not depend on what any registration converged to — and overlap is the smaller of the two directional fractions of points with a partner within 0.5 m (LiDAR) or 1.0 m (radar).

  • Accumulating a window helps but does not rescue a non-overlapping pair: A2D2 SL–SR goes from 0.000 to 0.003 over five seconds of driving.
  • Across the 18 pairs where registration converged, the rank correlation between overlap and registration error is ρ = −0.897 (p = 4.7×10−7).
  • The four A2D2 pairs where registration found no consensus all sit at overlap ≤ 0.41, so including them could only strengthen the trend.
Note
The paper presents this relationship as a picture of two real local maps rather than as a correlation coefficient, on the grounds that a reader who has not seen the overlap definition cannot interpret the number. The measurement is kept here.
