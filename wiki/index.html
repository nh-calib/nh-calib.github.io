Every other page is a detailed account of one box in that split.

Properties worth knowing before reading further

  • No overlapping field of view is required. Sensors are never registered against each other; only their motion is compared.
  • No targets, no infrastructure. The vehicle's own turning motion is the calibration signal.
  • Modality-independent. Any front end that produces metric linear and angular velocity qualifies — LiDAR odometry, radar odometry, GNSS/INS, or wheel speed with an angular-rate source.
  • One-way pipeline. A1 → A5 → B3 with no feedback loop; changing an early setting invalidates everything after it.
  • Relative by construction. Results are stated against a reference sensor, which is also the gauge anchor and the fixed graph node.
  • Scale is assumed known. Sensors without metric translation, such as a monocular camera, are outside the scope of the method as described here.

Reading caveats

Caution
Numeric values on these pages are reference-implementation defaults for the reported configuration. Where the design note and the manuscript differ — for instance in solver initialisation — the page says so explicitly rather than silently picking one. Accuracy figures are stated with their sample basis; single-log numbers are not representative on their own.
